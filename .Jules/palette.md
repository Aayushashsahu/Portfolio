## 2024-10-24 - Form Label Association
**Learning:** Adding `for` and `id` attributes to form fields improves screen reader accessibility and increases the clickable area for users by creating a programmatic relationship between a label and its control.
**Action:** Always ensure explicit `for` and `id` bindings are implemented between the `<label>` and `<input>`/`<textarea>` elements in the custom `.fg` form group pattern.
