## 2024-10-25 - Explicit Form Label Association
**Learning:** Forms utilizing the `.fg` pattern require explicit `for` and `id` bindings between `<label class="flabel">` and `<input class="finput">` elements to ensure screen readers can accurately announce the fields and to improve hit area for users interacting with labels.
**Action:** Always add explicit `for` and `id` attributes to form fields in `index.html`.
