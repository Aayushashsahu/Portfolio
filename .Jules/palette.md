## 2026-07-28 - Explicit Form Label Bindings
**Learning:** The custom `.fg` form group pattern in this application lacks explicit `for` and `id` bindings between `<label class="flabel">` and `<input class="finput">`, which breaks screen reader accessibility and reduces the click target area.
**Action:** Always ensure explicit `for` and `id` bindings are implemented between labels and inputs when working with the custom `.fg` form group pattern in this application.
