## 2025-06-13 - Explicit Form Label Bindings for Accessibility
**Learning:** Implicit label associations (where inputs are just placed next to labels) are insufficient for good accessibility and UX. Specifically, screen readers might not associate the label with the input correctly, and more importantly, users cannot click on the label text to automatically focus the adjacent input field, which is a common and expected interaction pattern that improves usability, especially on mobile or for users with motor difficulties.
**Action:** Always ensure that form labels and their corresponding inputs are explicitly bound using the `for` attribute on the `<label>` and a matching `id` on the `<input>` or `<textarea>`. This creates a programmatically deterministic relationship that improves both accessibility and interaction (label clickability).
